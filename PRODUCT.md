# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

DuckProject is created by a second-year ICT student at Ubon Ratchathani University. The portal is for anyone who needs to find and open its applications.

SCQuizz is used mainly at activities with first-year students and younger visitors attending university camps. Participants send questions from phones; organizers read and respond to messages in real time, usually on a computer.


## Product Purpose

Bring different useful applications together behind one easy-to-understand portal. Each application serves its own audience and task.

## Positioning

The name DuckProject reflects the Thai idea of a “duck”: someone who can do many things. The site intentionally supports more than one purpose.

## Operating Context

Users find an application through the portal. Access depends on each application's permissions. SCQuizz supports live questions and answers during activities.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing application: Django, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Portal: simple, readable, and usable on phones and computers. Make each app recognizable with its logo.
- SCQuizz: prioritize phone use for sending questions and computer use for reading and managing them.
- Use Thai for interface labels and guidance; retain English only where necessary for product or technical terms.
- Preserve existing routes, permissions, data, and working app behavior during the redesign.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the DuckProject name and its versatile, multipurpose meaning. The applications may have distinct information density and workflows within a coherent product family.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the portal and SCQuizz. The user supplied a visual reference for a restrained portal with clear app rows and asked for app logos. Do not invent testimonials, customer claims, or usage metrics.

## Product Principles

- Make the portal calm and easy for anyone to scan.
- Make the next action obvious within each application.
- Adapt layout and information density to each application's real users.
- Use readable Thai and clear controls across phone and computer sizes.
